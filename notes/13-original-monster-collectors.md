# 13 — Original Monster Collectors (Legal Peers)

> Games that already did "Pokémon, but we can sell it." What to steal without copying their IP either.

## Why this chapter exists

If we only study fangames, we will accidentally rebuild Pokémon with extra steps. These titles are the competitive set for a *shippable* creature RPG.

---

## Cassette Beasts (Bytten Studio, Godot)

**Thesis:** You record monsters onto cassette tapes and *become* them. Any two can fuse mid-battle.

Why it is the current genre champion:

- Fusion is a verb, not a menu. It changes the fight *now*
- Recording-as-catching is diegetic and legally distinct
- Adult-friendly writing without Reborn cruelty
- Best-in-class soundtrack
- Open-ish island, visible monsters, strong art direction
- Steam Workshop / mod culture

Steal:

- Catching fantasy that is not a ball
- Fusion as a Bond-cost emergency in our system
- Tone: warm, weird, sad at the edges
- Mod hooks

Do not steal: their specific tape/80s aesthetic (they own that look now).

---

## Coromon

**Thesis:** Pokémon-shaped campaign with a stamina meter on every move.

Steal:

- Stamina / fatigue as a way to stop "same move 30 times"
- Potential / IV transparency
- Skin / cosmetic system
- Very complete QoL

Caution: it is the closest to a "Pokéclone." We must be *more* thematically distinct than Coromon to avoid being "the other Coromon."

---

## Monster Sanctuary

**Thesis:** Metroidvania map + 3v3 synergy combat.

Steal:

- Monster abilities as exploration keys (our pact verbs)
- Team synergy as the skill expression (not just type chart)
- NG+ and randomizer as first-class

Caution: 2D platforming is a different production. Do not become a platformer unless we mean it. Take the *gating* idea into top-down.

---

## Temtem

**Thesis:** Always-online MMO with 2v2 from the first fight, full co-op campaign.

Steal:

- Co-op as a mode
- Doubles as the "real" battle
- Breeding/TV stats as a known live-service pitfall (do not copy the grind)

Caution: always-online killed goodwill. Our story must work offline.

---

## Nexomon / Nexomon Extinction

**Thesis:** Big original dex, self-aware writing, classic structure.

Steal:

- Volume is possible if art is stylized and consistent
- Humor in NPC text
- "We know you have played this genre"

Caution: combat depth is not their moat. Do not copy shallow movepools.

---

## Palworld

**Thesis:** Survival craft + creature labor + guns.

Steal:

- Creatures that *work* in the world (our ecology, not slave labor unless we are making a satire ending)
- Base/hub fantasy
- The market proof that non-Pokémon creature games can be gigantic

Caution: IP-adjacent creature design is how you get lawyers. Also, survival loops can eat the RPG.

---

## Siralim Ultimate

**Thesis:** 1,200+ monsters, theorycraft, grind-as-the-game.

Steal:

- Data depth, build identity, almost roguelike
- Honesty that the overworld is a menu

Caution: not our 1.0. A post-game "Reliquary" mode could be Siralim-shaped.

---

## Monster Hunter Stories 2

**Thesis:** Rock-paper-scissors hearts + riding + kinship combo.

Steal:

- Kinship / Bond as a super
- Monsties that feel like *monsters*, not pets
- Expeditions

---

## Shin Megami Tensei / Persona

**Thesis:** Negotiation, moon phases, demons as ideologies.

Steal:

- Talk as a catch method (directly relevant to attunement)
- Press-turn or equivalent resource (maybe later)
- Demons that will not join a chaotic player — *alignment*

This is the closest mechanical cousin to "pacts that can refuse you."

---

## Dragon Quest Monsters / Jade Cocoon / Monster Rancher

Older grammar:

- **Synthesis / fusion breeding** (DQM, Jade Cocoon)
- **Care as gameplay** (Rancher)
- **CD-scanned identities** (Rancher — not something we can do, but "every object has a spirit" is ECHOES-compatible)

Steal the *care* loop in miniature: Bond decays if you only use an Echo as a weapon.

---

## Yo-kai Watch / Ni no Kuni / Digimon

- Yo-kai: everyday-urban spirits. Very close to our "frequencies of human life"
- Ni no Kuni: spectacle and charm. Production we cannot match
- Digimon: evolution as a path tree, not a ladder. **Retune trees** for us

---

## Moonstone Island / Ooblets / farm hybrids

Everlasting Orchard in the fangame scene, Moonstone commercially.

Steal: a home that grows. Do not make farming mandatory for battle progression.

---

## PokeRogue (genre, not IP-clean)

As a *structure*: short run, meta unlocks, endlessly discussable.

Our "Rogue Frequency" mode: 40-minute seeded runs using official Echo data. Ships after 1.0, uses the same kernel. This is how we keep a Discord alive.

We aim **upper-right of center**: atmosphere + enough mechanical teeth that Tactician mode is real. Not Kaizo. Not a walking simulator with pets.

## Feature board we are allowed to combine

These are genre-public ideas:

- Type chart
- Party of N
- Exploration gates from creature verbs
- Fusion
- Stamina
- Negotiation
- Fields / terrains
- Co-op
- Roguelike mode
- Visible overworld creatures
- Difficulty modes

The *combination* plus original fiction is the game.

**ECHOES combination:**

> Negotiation-catch (SMT) + Fields-as-places (Reborn) + Ecology consequences (ours) + Cassette-like warmth + Unbound QoL + optional Temtem co-op + optional Rogue mode.
