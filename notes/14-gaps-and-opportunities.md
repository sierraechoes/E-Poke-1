# 14 — Gaps and Opportunities

> What official Pokémon, ROM hacks, fangames, and legal peers still do not do well — and therefore where a new game can be the best.

## Gaps in official Pokémon (2025–2026)

| Gap | Evidence | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Difficulty options | Still mostly "exp share on/off" | Unbound already won this; we must match |
| Story courage | BW was 2010 | Adult-capable, not adult-edgy |
| World persistence | Wilds reset, actions do not matter | Ecology that remembers |
| Creature as more than a stat block | Friendship is a number | Bond that refuses, flees, protects |
| Battle vs overworld split | Interruptive fights | Visible wilds + optional overworld attune |
| Gimmick churn | Megas left, came back; Z, Dyna, Tera | One gimmick tied to theme |
| Multiplayer campaign | Almost nonexistent | Optional 2P |
| Performance / finish | SV launch | Smaller map, higher density |
| Modding | None official | Data-first OSS |
| Moral complexity of catching | Balls are toys | Harvest vs pact |
| Useful "bad" Pokémon | Power creep | Role grid + fields make niches |
| Post-game as a second thesis | Often a legendary list | Facility *and* ecology epilogue |

## Gaps in ROM hacks

- Still Nintendo's world, legally trapped
- GBA limits: UI for complex fields is cramped
- "All mons" as a substitute for design
- Stories that are either official-clone or grimdark
- Little real ecology; routes are tables
- Almost no true co-op
- Accessibility (color, remaps, text size) is inconsistent

## Gaps in Essentials fangames

- Same RMXP face
- Same 8-gym skeleton with a coat of "cults"
- Abandoned demos
- Fields (Reborn) are under-copied relative to how good they are
- Few living worlds
- Online is rare and fragile
- Legal doom at success

## Gaps in Cassette Beasts et al.

- Cassette Beasts fusion can overshadow creature identity ("I fuse everything into the same gods")
- Coromon is mechanically close to Pokémon, low distinct fiction
- Temtem grind + always online
- Sanctuary is a platformer first
- Nobody has fully married **Reborn fields + SMT negotiation + a reacting ecology + Unbound-level finish**

That intersection is empty. That is the opportunity.

## The "most advanced" idea is not more types

Advanced does **not** mean:

- 40 types
- 4,000 creatures
- Open world the size of Paldea
- Action combat that abandons tactics
- LLM-generated dialogue
- Blockchain
- "AI generated Fakemon"

Advanced means:

1. **Systems that talk to each other** (battle field *is* the overworld biome *is* the story state)
2. **Creatures with agency**
3. **A world that can get worse because of you**
4. **Tools for other people to extend it**
5. **A finished, performant, accessible game**

## What "fully immersive" should mean here

Not VR. Not 8K grass.

Immersive =

- Continuity of space (day/night, weather, return hooks)
- Continuity of consequence (ecology, reputation, who will pact)
- Continuity of character (Echoes you remember, NPCs with jobs)
- Sensory identity (music, palettes, UI chrome that is *ours*)
- No menu that feels like it belongs to another game

PLA was the official high-water mark for immersion. Reborn for battle-as-place. Cassette Beasts for vibe. We take those three.

## Why open source is part of the idea, not a license footer

The fangame scene's knowledge is trapped in Discord dumps, dead forums, and ROM patches. An open Godot creature RPG with a clean data schema could become what Essentials *should* have been if it were legally allowed to be infrastructure.

Success metric beyond "a good game":

- A stranger can add an Echo with a folder of resources and no engine fork
- A teacher can use the battle kernel to teach design
- A total conversion can exist without asking us

That is how this project outlives a C&D that will never come, because there is nothing to C&D.
