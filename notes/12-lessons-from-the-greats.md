# 12 — Lessons from the Greats

> Cross-project design rules extracted from the hacks, fangames, and official peaks.

## Product lessons

1. **Finish a smaller game.** Uranium's 150 Fakemon shipped. Wack's 4,000 did not make it a better game. Unbound shipped. A hundred "new region" demos did not.
2. **One thesis per project.** Fields (Reborn). Open order (Crystal Clear). Competitive puzzles (Radical Red). Fusion (Infinite Fusion / Cassette Beasts). Nuclear history (Uranium). If we cannot put the thesis on a box, we have a feature pile.
3. **Difficulty is a mode, not a personality.** Unbound's four sliders opened the audience without watering the hard path.
4. **QoL is respect.** Reusable TMs, no HM slaves, run indoors, fast text, seen-indicators. Players interpret missing QoL as contempt.
5. **Original music is identity.** Unbound, Cassette Beasts, Reborn. Silence or ripped MIDI is how a game feels unofficial.
6. **Post-launch tuning beats pre-launch perfection.** Reborn's passwords. Radical Red's ongoing balance. Ship, then listen.
7. **The demo must be the first chapter, not a bedroom.**

## Battle lessons

8. **Limited options create identity.** Reborn's early dex makes every catch an event. National dex from route 1 makes every catch a blur.
9. **Place is the unused modifier.** Weather was the hint. Fields are the sentence.
10. **Make every species sayable.** Drayano / Radical Red / Tectonic: buff the weak in *data*. Do not lecture the player into using them.
11. **AI should be declared.** Players accept losing to a boss that is "smart." They rage at an AI that reads a switch it should not know.
12. **Items in battle are a design layer.** Radical Red's refillable berries make items part of the puzzle. Official "one Sitrus, then go buy more" is busywork.
13. **Doubles is the better advanced game.** Support singles first; do not paint ourselves into a 1v1-only data model.
14. **Gimmicks stick when they are character design** (Megas), not multipliers (Z-Moves).

## World lessons

15. **Return hooks.** Unbound and HGSS: you revisit with new verbs. Hallway regions die after one pass.
16. **Towns need jobs.** If everyone is a trainer, nobody lives there.
17. **Gates should be verbs, not rocks.** Ride Pokémon / ADM / pact skills.
18. **Openness needs identity per biome.** Crystal Clear works because Johto already had identity. A blank open map does not.
19. **Visible creatures.** Let's Go / PLA / SV / Cassette Beasts. Invisible grass is legacy.

## Story lessons

20. **Mature ≠ grimdark.** Unbound's optional dark story is the commercial sweet spot. Reborn is the niche sweet spot. Pick one and be honest.
21. **Institutions are better villains than teams in matching shirts.**
22. **A rival who wins is a character.** A rival who loses 8–0 is a stat stick.
23. **Choice that changes who will pact with you** is more Pokémon than a cutscene flag.
24. **Humor keeps darkness playable.** Cassette Beasts, Unbound, good Nexomon writing.

## Production lessons

25. **Data tools are the real engine.** Studio vs PBS typos. Godot Resources + validators.
26. **Config flags beat forks.** pokeemerald-expansion's headers. Our difficulty and field toggles should be data.
27. **Community art needs a pipeline** (Infinite Fusion) or it is a graveyard of "we'll open submissions."
28. **Legal success condition is original IP.** Uranium is the ghost at this feast.
29. **Document as we go.** pret's wikis and the Essentials docs are why those ecosystems lived.
30. **Play the game every week.** Hacks rot when the author only adds features.

## Anti-lessons (things that look like success)

- "All 1000 Pokémon" as a feature bullet
- 21 starters
- 18 gyms before a playable Act I
- Mid-battle 4,000-word lore dumps
- Randomizer as the only replay value
- Announcing a 2029 scope in a 2026 repo
- Edgy passwords as a substitute for content warnings + design
- Pixel-perfect official clones of Kanto "for nostalgia"

## The ten commandments for E-Poke-1

1. Original IP only.
2. One thesis: **living Fields + pacts that can refuse you.**
3. 140 Echoes, not 800.
4. Vertical slice before lore bible inflation.
5. Battle kernel testable without the overworld.
6. Difficulty modes from file create.
7. No HM jail.
8. Every route has a Field and a reason to return.
9. Finish Act I as a public demo.
10. If a feature does not serve the thesis or the QoL table-stakes list, it waits.
